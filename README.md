# ESG

 							
About this project							
							
Purpose	Build a sustainability readiness system for a fictional Irish DIY retailer. Track supplier evidence, products, packaging, environmental KPIs, regulatory topics, costs and corrective actions.						
							
Owner	Sagar Malik. Portfolio demonstration inspired by the green colour direction of Woodie's. It is not a Woodie's operational workbook or an official brand template.						
							
Data boundary	Five suppliers, five products, eight packaging components and one store with twelve months of 2025 activity. Existing activity and supplier inputs have been retained.						
							
Review date	Dashboard C4 is an editable snapshot date. It starts at 27-Sep-2026. Changing it updates due statuses. Legal sources were checked on 27-Sep-2026.											
Current evidence	All five supplier claims remain Unverified. Descriptions such as technical sheet received are synthetic scenario text; no actual supplier documents were provided.						
							
Evidence readiness	Suppliers V4 counts records ready for approval divided by all supplier records. A claim needs verification, a real reference and current evidence before it is ready for a wording review.						
							
Weight data completion	Packaging R4 counts component rows with usable keys, material, component, weight and volume. Completeness does not mean that weights are verified.						
							
Recycled material	Verified recycled percentages stay blank until evidence confirms the percentage, packaging components and material source. Zero would mean a known absence of recycled content.						
							
Monthly calculations	Energy use multiplied by the relevant kg CO2/kWh factor gives kg CO2. Annual recycling is recycled waste divided by total waste over the same year.						
							
Emissions boundary	Current outputs are energy-related CO2 estimates. A full greenhouse-gas inventory needs a boundary, further sources and gases, and an appropriate electricity accounting method.						
							
Legal register	Six screening topics are included for the sample products. Source dates, conditional application dates and internal tasks are kept separate. Scope and readiness require review.						
							
Costs	Quantity multiplied by rate gives base cost. A shared 10% planning contingency is added. Staff time is included as a resource cost. Actual amounts are unknown.						
							
How to update	Edit amber cells. Formula cells are pale green. Use dropdown values. Keep IDs unique. Record an evidence reference before changing a verification or closure decision.						
							
Adding records	Add records inside the relevant Excel Table and fill its formulas. Supplier, product and packaging lookups/summaries allow rows 2 to 501. Extend fixed action, cost and monthly ranges when their record counts change.						
							
Date handling	Dates are numeric Excel dates displayed as dd-mmm-yyyy or mmm-yyyy. Supplier requests dated 28-Sep-2026 show Scheduled when reviewed on 27-Sep-2026.						
							
Action closure	A Closed status requires a completion date and a closure evidence reference. Supplier action fields read Suppliers, so update the source instead of overwriting links.						
							
Theme	Forest green #245B36; leaf green #6D963D; pale green #EAF2E8; amber inputs #FFF2CC; red exceptions #FBE7E5. Arial provides a consistent font across cells and charts.
